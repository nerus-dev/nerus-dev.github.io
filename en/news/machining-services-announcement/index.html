Component machining: archived announcement

Turning and milling of components and blanks.

23 March 2017 · Historical material

The announcement lists surface and end-face machining, boring, threading, grooves, knurling and hole finishing. Milling includes planes, slots and keyways.

Examples include barrels, rods, shafts, pistons, bushings, non-standard fasteners and hydraulic valve spools. The machining service page provides the current demonstration enquiry route.
