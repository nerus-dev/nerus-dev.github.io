Hoses and fittings

Hydraulic hose assemblies, hose by length and end fittings.

Add to request
Product illustration. An AI-generated illustration of the general product design. This is not a photograph of company work, stock or a specific catalogue item.

High-pressure hoses are supplied as assemblies with fittings or by length. The range includes MANULI, Teknohose, SEMPERIT, SEL, Svarog and Parker Hannifin.

End fittings include hose ends, connectors and crimp ferrules. Connection types include DK, DKO, SF3000, SF6000, JIC, JIS, BSP, ORFS and Banjo.

Selection starts with a part number or sample, hose size, length and connections. See the hose assembly service for manufacturing enquiries.
