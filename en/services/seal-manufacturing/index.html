How the work happens

Profile, material and manufacture

A seal is selected for its component and operating conditions. An order starts by identifying its type and material using the profile and material catalogues.

Identify the function and profile

We distinguish rod and piston seals, wipers, support and guide rings, and static and rotary seals. The profile catalogue identifies the type for the required component.

Details and conditions

The published range includes rod and piston seals, support and guide rings, static seals, O-rings, symmetrical profiles and rotary seals.

The source describes production for special requirements, including metallurgy, forging and injection-moulding machinery, and high or low temperatures. Permissible conditions depend on material and design.

For an enquiry, identify the profile and material from the catalogues and provide dimensions and the working medium. Material and profile catalogues are available as separate Russian PDF originals.

The facilities description mentions SEAL JET. Historical certification statements are not presented here as proof of a current certificate.
