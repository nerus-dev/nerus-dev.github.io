Details and conditions

The source specifies barrel diameters up to 500 mm and lengths up to 8000 mm. The repair facilities are in Yekaterinburg.

The cylinder is first dismantled and inspected. The findings determine which components can be restored and which need replacement.

Work may involve seals, the piston rod, barrel, piston, guide bushing and mounting eyes, with turning and welding where required.

Assembly is followed by pressure and bench testing. The source also describes removal, transport and refitting to machinery; arrangements depend on the individual order.

An initial enquiry should identify the cylinder make and type, damage, photographs and available dimensions. Lead time, price, transport and warranty terms are not calculated automatically.
