We use the catalogue or part number. If no number is available, a hose sample or drawing provides the starting information.

Details and conditions

TekhSnab describes manufacturing wire-braided and spiral-wire hose assemblies with fittings, as well as hydraulic hose repairs.

Identification can use a catalogue number, part number, sample or drawing. Useful selection details include length, hose size, connection types and orientation, and operating parameters.

The source lists DK, DKO, SF3000, SF6000, JIC, JIS, BSP, ORFS and Banjo connections. A listed type does not establish availability of a particular configuration.

The catalogue provides an overview of hoses and fittings. Price and manufacturing lead time depend on the enquiry specifications.
