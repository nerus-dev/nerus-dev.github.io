Details and conditions

The facilities page describes barrel and shaft machining and the manufacture of rods, pistons, guide bushings, non-standard bolts and other parts from drawings or sketches.

Turning operations include external and end-face machining, boring, grooves, threading, knurling, counterboring and reaming.

Milling covers flat and inclined surfaces, angled, rectangular and T-slots, and keyways.

The facilities page lists DIP 300, an extended-bed DIP 500, drilling, universal milling, vertical and horizontal boring machines, CNC and SEAL JET. A 2022 archive separately names 16K20 and 1A64 (DIP 400); these are different published lists, not a verified current equipment inventory.

Attach a drawing, material designation, dimensions and quantity. Feasibility, tolerances and price require assessment of the particular component.
