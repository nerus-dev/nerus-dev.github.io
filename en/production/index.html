Equipment and capabilities

The machining department primarily makes and restores components for machinery repairs. The source also describes work from external customers’ drawings and sketches, and design of non-standard equipment.

The facilities list includes a DIP 300 lathe, an extended-bed DIP 500, drilling and universal milling machines, vertical and horizontal boring machines, CNC and SEAL JET.

Operations include barrel boring, shaft machining, rods, pistons and bushings, slots, bearing seats, non-standard fasteners and hydraulic valve components.

The photographs are material published by the company. They are not an independent verification of the present equipment inventory or condition.
