Documents and catalogues

Seven original PDF files and an XLS size workbook.

The library includes KASTAS, material, seal-profile and mounting-eye catalogues, an extended shaft-seal size workbook and three questionnaires. All originals are in Russian; they are not English translations.

The cylinder, gland and piston questionnaires are archived forms retaining an old Lenina Street address, the ts-ekb.ru domain and historical company details. They do not define the company’s current contacts.

Use the local form to prepare a demonstration enquiry; original documents remain reference material. Downloading a catalogue does not establish current certification or product availability.
