Running gear maintenance: start with a clear symptom description

Running gear concerns can appear as knocks, vibration or changes in vehicle behaviour. A sound alone cannot identify the faulty component: when and under which conditions it occurs matters. Assessment should consider the particular vehicle’s design and the reported symptoms.

This CARBONLAB service area covers passenger-car running gear maintenance and repair. A confirmed list of individual operations is not yet presented on the website. During the initial discussion, clarify whether the particular work is available for your model and which checks are needed before the repair scope can be established.

Describe previous repairs, impacts and the conditions in which the problem occurs. Parts selection, work scope and cost are agreed before work begins. If the vehicle’s behaviour is clearly unsafe, do not continue driving; arrange safe transport or assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Which specific repairs are available?

Ask the manager about individual operations and compatibility with your vehicle. The general website description does not mean that every suspension or related-component repair is available for all models.

What should I describe if I hear a knocking sound?

Explain whether the sound occurs over bumps, during turns, under braking or at a particular speed. Its approximate location, when the problem began and any recent work are also useful.

Can you identify which part needs replacement in advance?

A telephone description alone cannot reliably determine this. Appropriate checks are needed first, followed by a discussion of possible actions, necessary parts and cost.
