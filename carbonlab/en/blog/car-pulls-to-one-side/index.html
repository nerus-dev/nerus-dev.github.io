Car pulling to one side: more than wheel alignment

A pull to the left or right needs tyre, alignment and related component checks. Understand what to ask before ordering an adjustment.

Images are illustrative and do not show CARBONLAB’s actual workshop or completed work.

A pull is a symptom

A vehicle pulling sideways does not establish that wheel alignment alone will fix it. Unequal tyre pressures, tyre condition, suspension and steering need consideration. Explain whether the change followed an impact, a tyre replacement or developed gradually. Even an off-centre steering wheel is not the complete complaint. Ask for the cause of the handling change to be established rather than choosing a familiar operation from a short description.

Record what you already noticed

Recall whether the pull occurred during normal travel, braking you had already performed or on a particular road. Mention recent tyre work and visible damage observed while parked. Tyre pressure should follow the vehicle manufacturer's requirements, not the maximum printed on the tyre sidewall. Arrange a professional check if you are unsure. Do not release the steering wheel or perform deliberate braking experiments to test the direction.

Before an alignment adjustment

Ask whether the tyres and suspension condition have been checked and which measurements support an adjustment. Damaged or badly worn components may prevent an alignment alone from addressing the cause. Request the measurement results and an explanation of what changed. A pull specifically during braking should not automatically be treated as an ordinary alignment issue. The conclusion must reflect the circumstances of your complaint, not just a general adjustment recommendation.

Choose safe assistance when needed

A sudden strong pull, reduced steering control, damaged tyre or braking problem calls for a safe stop and assistance. Establish whether driving is safe before travelling to a workshop under your own power. Give CARBONLAB the symptom history and confirm which work is available. Do not repeatedly approve the same adjustment without an explanation for a persistent pull. The outcome should be assessed against the original handling complaint.
