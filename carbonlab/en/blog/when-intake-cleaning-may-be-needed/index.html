How can you tell if the intake needs cleaning?

Why similar symptoms can have different causes.

Draft awaiting technical review before publication.

Images are illustrative and do not show CARBONLAB’s actual workshop or completed work.

A symptom is not a diagnosis

An uneven idle, misfires or changes in response can relate to several systems. Driving feel alone cannot reliably establish the presence of carbon deposits.

Prepare the information

Provide the engine model, mileage, service history, conditions in which the symptom appears and any known fault codes. This helps establish a sensible order of checks.

Decide after assessment

If inspection confirms significant deposits and other causes have been excluded, cleaning can be discussed. Its scope and expected result should be explained before work begins.

Uneven running and changes in response can have different causes. The need for work and its scope depend on an assessment of the particular vehicle.
